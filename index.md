🌍 What We Do

Nomad Visa Planner is a modern tool designed for travelers, digital nomads, and long-stay explorers.
We help users instantly understand visa rules, calculate stay limits, and plan multi-country trips without overstaying.

Our mission is simple:

Make global mobility clearer, safer, and easier for everyone.

🔧 Core Features
1. Visa & Entry Checker

Enter your passport + destination → get:

Visa-free days

eVisa / Visa on Arrival requirements

Maximum stay length

Cooling-off rules

Multi-entry policies

90/180 compliance (for Schengen)
All verified and updated weekly.

2. Multi-Country Stay Planner

Plan multiple destinations.
Our system calculates:

Total legal stay duration

Overstay risks

Which country requires extension

How to restructure your itinerary

Perfect for long-term nomads.

3. 90/180 Schengen Calculator

The hardest travel rule, solved.
Upload your past trips → we tell you:

How many days you still have

Which dates are safe to re-enter

Rolling window analysis

Overlapping-trip detection

4. Travel Affiliate Integrations

We partner with flight and accommodation platforms to offer:

Flight options

Hotel recommendations

Travel insurance suggestions

All via official partner programs.
